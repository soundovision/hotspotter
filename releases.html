What's new

1.0.3

25 November 2026

Hot Spotter 1.0.3 for Mac and Windows. Easier to read, alerts you can tell apart, and a simpler first screen.

  • A simpler first screen. Drop an album anywhere on it, or use Analyze Folder… and Analyze Files…. Your audio files are never changed.
  • Recent albums. Analyze ▾ → Recent lists your last 10 albums. Click one to analyze it again.
  • Easier to read. Bigger text, plainer type for sentences, bigger click areas on small controls, and tooltips when you move through the app with the keyboard.
  • Alerts you can tell apart. Clip now has its own square mark, and Loud and Quiet stay round. The Overview gives its counts once, in one line (“2 loud”, “3 quiet”, “2 clip”). Click a count to show only those tracks. If tracks changed since your last mix, the line says how many. Click it to see what changed.
  • Ignored clips stay in view. They turn gray instead of disappearing, and they stay under Clip with a hollow mark.
  • Color-blind palette. The Accessible colors were rebuilt for color-blind eyes and renamed Color-blind: amber for loud, blue for quiet, gray for in range, red-orange for clip.
  • Track Details puts the answer first. The track's suggestion sits right under its name, and each control sits beside what it changes, so the Spectrum sits higher. The Spectrum has its title back, with a Shape | Compare switch.
  • Exclude is an A/B switch. On the Overview, turn it off to see the album with every track counted, and on again to bring your exclusions back.
  • References compare everything. Picking a Reference turns on both loudness and Spectrum comparing. Going back to Album average turns both off.
  • Long names read better. They shorten in the middle, not at the end, so two names that differ only at the end look different. Hover one to see it whole. In a wide window the tracklist grows so names show in full.
  • Mark a value as seen. Click a colored Gain, Bass or Sub value in Track Details and it stops being highlighted until you quit. Bass and Sub are now colored in the tracklist too.
  • Spot Markers. Click the Spectrum to mark a frequency. S always marks the timeline. Only a marker's dot opens its note or drags it.
  • What the Readings Mean shows each reading next to a picture of it in the app.
  • Screen readers get headings and landmarks, and the tracklist is one Tab stop (use the arrow keys inside it). Windows High Contrast keeps the loudness bars, alert dots and Gain arrows.
  • The Spot Report counts tracks with an alert, like the header: a track that runs loud and clips counts once.
  • Fixes: help windows open at their normal size on every screen, the header no longer moves while you rename the album, and the header is calmer.

1.0.2

11 November 2026

Hot Spotter 1.0.2 for Mac and Windows.

  • Clearer Alerts. Loud, Quiet and Clip pills sit in the tracklist, the header's alerts list, the Overview and the Spot Report. Click one to filter the list.
  • Alerts in the Spot Report. See clips, loud, and quiet tracks in the Spot Report. Tap “Hide Alerts” to show only your notes.
  • Add Tracks to an Analysis. Add songs to an open album from Analyze ▾ → Add Tracks… or by dropping files on the report. Compare tracks across folders or grow a comparison while you work!
  • Better Control. The new dropdown lets you load a reference or manual LUFS target, and allows quick switching between each.
  • Faster on big albums. Scrolling, resizing and switching views stay smooth with hundreds of tracks, and a “Refresh Analysis” only measures files that change.
  • Exclude works everywhere. An excluded track stays quiet under a reference or a target LUFS.
  • Easier to read. Gain now shows as an arrow, to tell you to turn a track up or down. Skipped files read as a notice instead of an alert.
  • Units in Track Details. I can’t believe I forgot to add this earlier.
  • New Import Buttons. Now you can analyze both Folders and Files right from the start screen.
  • Views. In Track Details, hit the new View button to customize which stats about your track will show. Views are now consistent across the tracklist and details areas.
  • Windows: Fixed some Windows-specific bugs and keyboard shortcuts.

1.0.1

30 October 2026

Hot Spotter 1.0.1 for Mac and Windows.

  • Zoom the whole app. View → Zoom In, Zoom Out and Actual Size (⌘+, ⌘−, ⌘0). Hot Spotter remembers your zoom.
  • Spot Markers, faster. Press S to mark the moment that's playing, or hover the Spectrum and press S to mark a frequency. Right-click a waveform or the Spectrum to mark exactly where you point.
  • The Spectrum is always on. A color key now sits under it, and when it can't compare yet it tells you what to do: add a track, or load a reference straight from the link.
  • Better references. Turn a reference off and on without removing it, and choose whether it compares Spectrum, Loudness or both. Opening a new album starts without the last album's reference.
  • Mix Suggestions. Dismiss one with ✕, then bring it back with Restore Suggestions. A one-track album gets suggestions when a reference is loaded.
  • Cleaner starting views. The Overview opens in Classic bars with LUFS and Length; Tracks shows waveforms with LUFS, Length and Dynamics. Your views reset to these once.
  • Clearer wording throughout, including Analyze Folder and Refresh Analysis.
  • Optional usage reports, still off unless you turn them on, now include your country and which license you have. See the Privacy page.
